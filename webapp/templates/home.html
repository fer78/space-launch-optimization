Executive Summary

This project analyzes historical SpaceX launches to identify the operational variables that most influence mission success.

The solution combines exploratory data analysis, geospatial visualization, and machine learning to estimate the probability of success for future missions.

The final application transforms the technical analysis into an executive dashboard designed to support data-driven decisions.
