Executive Summary

This project analyzes historical SpaceX launches to identify the operational factors that influence mission success and to build predictive models capable of estimating the probability of a successful launch.

The solution combines exploratory data analysis, geospatial visualization, and machine learning to provide actionable insights for decision-makers.
